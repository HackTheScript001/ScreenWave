What's new in 1.0.0
- First version of ScreenWave: speeds up Samsung Tizen TVs by removing the preloaded apps you don't use, through the TV's Developer Mode.
- Connect over your home network, or with a cable straight from your PC to the TV – the TV never needs the internet.
- Lists the TV's apps, separates the ones that can be removed from Samsung's system apps (which are never touched), suggests the usual unused ones, and confirms each removal.
- Step-by-step speed-up settings checklist, a block list to stop removed apps coming back, and a finish-up guide to turn Developer Mode off again.
- Fully offline: ScreenWave never connects to the internet and sends nothing about you or your TV anywhere.

How to install
Download ScreenWave-x.y.z-Setup.exe below and run it.
